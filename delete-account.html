Delete Your Account

Chronicat: Cat Health Tracker — developed by Pixoro Apps

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Chronicat and sign in
  2. Go to Settings → Manage account
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm

Your account and the data listed below are deleted immediately — no waiting period.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the address associated with your account:

Email: pixoro.apps@gmail.com

Include the subject line "Delete my Chronicat account". We process requests within 30 days.

Clear your on-device cache (keep your account and cloud data)

Your cats and timeline events are stored on our cloud backend and synced to your device. Settings → Manage account → Sign out clears the copy cached on this device (including your locally-stored AI chat history and care reminder settings; photos you attached in chats may stay in the app's private storage until you uninstall the app) — it does not delete your cats or events from your account. The next time you sign in, your cats and events will reappear.

To actually delete your cats and timeline events, delete individual cats from within the app, or delete your account entirely (below) — there is currently no option to wipe all cat/event data while keeping the account.

What gets deleted

DataDeleted?
Account (email, password, profile)Yes, immediately

Reports you sent about AI repliesYes, deleted with your account
Subscription record at RevenueCatYes, deleted with your account
Usage analytics and crash reports (linked to your account ID)Not deleted automatically — kept by Google (Firebase) and Amplitude under their retention settings. Email us and we'll ask them to delete the data linked to your account ID
Purchase records (App Store, Google Play)Retained by Apple / Google per their policies and financial recordkeeping requirements. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — cancel it in the App Store or Google Play
